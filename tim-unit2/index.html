1. Parties and Premises

This Lease is between 9222 N 6th, LLC or Pilar Polanco ("Landlord"), acting through its manager and authorized agent Arthur Aguilar, and Timothy Higgins ("Tenant"). Amy Imbriale ("Guarantor") guarantees Tenant's obligations under the attached Guaranty Addendum. The Roommate / Sublease Addendum and the Guaranty Addendum are part of this Lease. Landlord rents to Tenant, for residential use only, the Premises listed above in their present condition, together with the parking described in Section 10.

2. Term, Early Possession, and Renewal

The Lease starts December 1, 2026 and ends November 30, 2027. Either party should give at least 30 days' written notice before the end date if not renewing. If Tenant stays with Landlord's consent and no new lease is signed, the tenancy continues month-to-month on the same terms, and either party may end it with at least 30 days' written notice before the next rent due date (A.R.S. § 33-1375). Any rent change on a renewal or month-to-month tenancy requires at least 30 days' written notice. Holding over without consent is governed by A.R.S. § 33-1375(C).

Early Possession: Tenant may take possession of the Premises beginning Friday, November 27, 2026, at no additional charge, once the full move-in amount has been received. Rent begins December 1, 2026. Tenant is responsible for utilities in Tenant's name, including electric, beginning December 1, 2026. All other terms of this Lease apply during early possession.

3. Rent and Payment

  • Rent is $1,300.00 per month, due in advance on the 1st and delinquent on the 2nd.
  • Payment method: Rent is paid by Zelle to Landlord's manager, Arthur Aguilar, at Zelle phone number (623) 206-7885, or by another method Landlord names in writing. Landlord will give a written receipt for any cash payment. Payments may be made by Guarantor on Tenant's behalf.
  • Move-in payment: The total move-in amount of $4,550.00 shown in the Move-In Costs table is paid by Zelle at signing. Keys and possession are given only after the full amount has been received.
  • Late fee: if rent is not paid within 3 days of the due date, Tenant owes $50.00 plus $10.00 per day after that until paid.
  • Returned payment: $100.00 for any payment that is returned, reversed, or declined for any reason. After a returned payment, Landlord may require certified funds.
  • Accepting a late or partial payment does not waive Landlord's right to full, on-time rent.

4. Prepaid Rent

At signing, Tenant pays the first month's rent for December 2026. In addition, Tenant voluntarily elects to prepay rent for January 2027 and February 2027 ($2,600.00), as A.R.S. § 33-1321(A) permits. This payment is prepaid rent only. It is applied to January and February 2027 rent and is not held as security. The next rent payment is due March 1, 2027.

5. Security Deposit and Fees

  • Tenant pays a $650.00 refundable security deposit at signing.
  • Nonrefundable fees: None. Any fee not stated in this Lease as nonrefundable is refundable.
  • Within 14 days, excluding weekends and legal holidays, after the tenancy ends, possession is returned, and Tenant demands the deposit, Landlord will mail the balance to the forwarding address given by Tenant with an itemized list of deductions (A.R.S. § 33-1321(D)). Deductions are limited to unpaid rent and charges and damage beyond normal wear and tear.
  • Tenant may be present at the move-out inspection.

6. Utilities

Water, sewer, and trash are included in rent and paid by Landlord. Tenant pays all other utilities, including electricity (APS) and gas (Southwest Gas) if the Premises are served by gas, plus any phone, internet, or television service, and sets them up in Tenant's name starting December 1, 2026.

7. Occupants, Guests, Roommate, and Subletting

Only Timothy Higgins and at most one (1) roommate approved by Landlord in writing under the attached Roommate / Sublease Addendum may live in the Premises, for a maximum of two (2) occupants. Guarantor does not reside in the Premises and is not an occupant. Guests may stay overnight only at Landlord's discretion, and Landlord may limit or end any guest's stay. Except for one approved roommate under that Addendum, there is no subletting, assignment, or additional occupant without Landlord's written consent.

8. Use of Premises and Conduct

The Premises are a private residence. Tenant and guests will not disturb neighbors, break any law, use or store illegal drugs, keep hazardous or explosive materials (beyond normal household products), or damage the property. Tenant will follow Landlord's reasonable written rules, which are part of this Lease. No waterbeds or liquid furniture without written consent.

Supervision visits: Tenant consents to lawful visits to the Premises by any officer responsible for Tenant's community supervision, as required by the terms of that supervision. Landlord will cooperate with reasonable lawful requests to verify Tenant's residence. Tenant is responsible for complying with any supervision condition about who may live with Tenant (see the Roommate / Sublease Addendum).

9. Pets and Smoking

  • No pets of any kind, even temporarily, unless a written pet addendum is signed by both parties. Service and assistance animals for a person with a disability are allowed as required by fair housing law, with no pet fee or pet rent.
  • No smoking or vaping of any product (including tobacco and marijuana) inside the unit or within 20 feet of doors and windows. Tenant pays the cost of removing smoke odor or residue.
Initials: Sections 7 to 9

10. Vehicles and Parking

  • Tenant is assigned Parking Space 2, the space directly next to Unit 1's space (Space 1 is the space closest to the building). Parking is only for Tenant and any approved roommate, and may not be rented or loaned to anyone else.
  • Vehicles must never block the ingress or egress of Landlord or any other resident or vehicle.
  • Vehicles must be operable, registered, and insured. Inoperable or abandoned vehicles may be towed at the owner's expense after notice as allowed by law.
  • No major mechanical work on site (minor upkeep like fluids or a tire change is fine). Tenant pays to clean oil or fluid stains.

11. Maintenance and Repairs

Landlord is responsible for:

  • All exterior maintenance (roof, exterior walls, grounds, parking, common areas).
  • Interior repairs costing more than $250.
  • Regardless of cost, every repair needed to keep the Premises fit and habitable (A.R.S. §§ 33-1324 and 33-1315): plumbing, water heater, heating and air conditioning, electrical, gas, roof and structural items, pests or bed bugs not caused by Tenant, and anything required by building, health, or safety codes. Landlord also supplies running water, reasonable hot water, and trash receptacles.

Tenant is responsible for:

  • Minor interior repairs and routine upkeep costing $250 or less that are not habitability or code items, such as light bulbs, smoke-detector batteries, minor caulking, loose knobs or towel bars, screens, blind slats, and drain clogs caused by Tenant's use.
  • A/C filters: Tenant replaces the HVAC filter (size 12 x 24 x 1) monthly, at Tenant's expense. Damage caused by neglected filters is Tenant's responsibility. Otherwise, Landlord remains responsible for A/C repairs.
  • Any damage caused by Tenant or guests, at any cost, beyond normal wear and tear (A.R.S. § 33-1341).
  • Keeping the Premises clean, safe, and sanitary, and promptly reporting any defect or dangerous condition. Tenant may be charged for added damage caused by a delay in reporting.

Rules for work: No gas, electrical, or structural work by Tenant. No alterations, painting, lock changes, or alarm installs without Landlord's written consent; Tenant gives Landlord a key to any approved new lock. Tenant will test smoke detectors monthly and never disable them.

12. Landlord Entry

Except in an emergency, Landlord will give at least two (2) days' notice and enter only at reasonable times to inspect, repair, or show the Premises (A.R.S. § 33-1343). Routine inspections are limited to once a month. Tenant will not unreasonably refuse entry, and Landlord will not use entry to harass Tenant. Tenant will tell Landlord in advance of any absence over 7 days (A.R.S. § 33-1373).

13. Insurance and Liability

Landlord's insurance does not cover Tenant's belongings or vehicles. Landlord is not responsible for loss of Tenant's property except loss caused by Landlord's negligence. Renter's insurance is strongly recommended.

14. Default, Abandonment, and Termination

  • Unpaid rent: 5-day written notice to pay or the Lease ends (A.R.S. § 33-1368(B)).
  • Breach affecting health and safety: 5-day written notice to fix.
  • Other material breach: 10-day written notice to fix.
  • Material and irreparable breach (such as violent or drug-related crime on the property): immediate termination as allowed by A.R.S. § 33-1368(A).
  • A material misstatement on Tenant's rental application is grounds for termination.
  • Abandonment is determined under A.R.S. § 33-1370, after the required posted and mailed notice. Left-behind property is handled under § 33-1370, including the 14-day holding period.
  • Landlord will not lock Tenant out, shut off utilities, or remove belongings to force a move (A.R.S. § 33-1367). If Tenant moves out early, Tenant owes rent until the end of the term or until the unit is re-rented, whichever comes first; Landlord will make reasonable efforts to re-rent.
  • If Landlord cannot deliver possession for reasons beyond Landlord's control, Tenant may terminate and receive back all money paid.

The Premises are approximately 760 square feet. If this Addendum conflicts with the Lease, this Addendum controls.

1. One roommate maximum. Tenant may share the Premises with no more than one (1) roommate ("Roommate") approved by Landlord under this Addendum, for a maximum of two (2) occupants in total. The money terms of the Lease, including rent, prepaid rent, and the security deposit, do not change. Once approved, the Roommate may stay for as long as Tenant wants the Roommate there, up to the end of Tenant's tenancy. Guest-stay limits do not apply to the approved Roommate.

2. Tenant is the master tenant. Tenant remains the only tenant under the Lease. Tenant and Guarantor remain jointly and fully liable for all rent, fees, charges, and damage under the Lease, regardless of what the Roommate pays or fails to pay Tenant.

3. No tenancy with Landlord. The Roommate pays Tenant, not Landlord. Landlord does not accept rent from the Roommate, and any payment Landlord receives is applied only to Tenant's account. The Roommate is not a tenant of Landlord, has no separate tenancy with Landlord, and has no rights against Landlord under the Lease. The Roommate may not move in until Landlord approves in writing. Landlord may decline a proposed Roommate for any lawful reason. Any replacement Roommate requires the same approval.

5. Rules apply to the Roommate. The Roommate must follow all terms and rules of the Lease, including: no pets, no smoking or vaping, no illegal activity, no additional occupants or long-term guests, and parking in Parking Space 2 only. Any violation by the Roommate or the Roommate's guests is a default by Tenant under the Lease and Arizona law.

6. Supervision conditions. Tenant is solely responsible for complying with any condition of Tenant's community supervision regarding who may live with Tenant, including any approval required for the Roommate. This does not change Section 8 of the Lease.

7. Guaranty. Guarantor's Guaranty Addendum expressly covers the Lease as modified by this Addendum, including all obligations, charges, and damage arising from any Roommate's occupancy or conduct.

Attachment B: Bed Bug Information (A.R.S. § 33-1319)

  • What they are: small, flat, reddish-brown insects about the size of an apple seed that hide in mattress seams, furniture, and cracks and bite at night.
