1. Parties and Premises

This Lease is between 9222 N 6th, LLC or Pilar Polanco ("Landlord"), acting through its manager and authorized agent Arthur Aguilar, and Michael Daniel Zepeda ("Tenant"). Landlord rents to Tenant, for residential use only, the Premises listed above in their present condition, together with the parking and storage shed rights described in Sections 10 and 11.

2. Term and Renewal

The Lease starts November 1, 2026 and ends October 31, 2027. Either party should give at least 30 days' written notice before the end date if not renewing. If Tenant stays with Landlord's consent and no new lease is signed, the tenancy continues month-to-month on the same terms, and either party may end it with at least 30 days' written notice before the next rent due date (A.R.S. § 33-1375). Any rent change on a renewal or month-to-month tenancy requires at least 30 days' written notice. Holding over without consent is governed by A.R.S. § 33-1375(C).

Early Access: Landlord grants Tenant access to the Premises beginning October 30, 2026 at no additional charge. Rent begins November 1, 2026, and Tenant is responsible for utilities in Tenant's name, including electric, beginning October 30, 2026. All other lease terms apply during early access.

3. Rent and Payment

  • Rent is \$1,300.00 per month, due in advance on the 1st and delinquent on the 2nd. No proration applies because the Lease starts on the 1st.
  • Pay to Landlord's manager, Arthur Aguilar, 14175 W Indian School Rd, B4-205, Goodyear, AZ 85395, by online payment, check, or cash (Landlord will give a written receipt for cash), or another method Landlord names in writing.
  • Late fee: if rent is not paid within 3 days of the due date, Tenant owes \$50.00 plus \$10.00 per day after that until paid.
  • Returned payment: \$100.00 for any check or payment returned for any reason. After a returned payment, Landlord may require money order or certified funds.
  • Accepting a late or partial payment does not waive Landlord's right to full, on-time rent.

4. Security Deposit and Fees

  • Tenant pays a \$650.00 refundable security deposit at signing. Arizona caps deposits plus prepaid rent at 1½ months' rent (A.R.S. § 33-1321).
  • Nonrefundable fees: None. Any fee not stated here as nonrefundable is refundable.
  • Within 14 days, excluding weekends and legal holidays, after the tenancy ends, possession is returned, and Tenant demands the deposit and gives a forwarding address, Landlord will mail the balance with an itemized list of deductions. Deductions are limited to unpaid rent and charges and damage beyond normal wear and tear.
  • Tenant may be present at the move-out inspection.

5. Utilities

Water, sewer, and trash are included in rent and paid by Landlord. Tenant pays all other utilities (such as electricity, gas, internet) and sets them up in Tenant's name.

6. Occupants, Guests, and Subletting

Only Michael Daniel Zepeda may live in the Premises. Guests may not stay more than 14 days total in any 6-month period without Landlord's written approval. Any additional occupant Landlord approves in writing adds \$866.00 per month in rent. No subletting or assignment (including the shed or parking) without Landlord's written consent.

7. Use of Premises and Conduct

The Premises are a private residence. Tenant may do office or paperwork for Tenant's business, but no customers, employees, inventory, or commercial operations on site. Tenant and guests will not disturb neighbors, break any law, use or store illegal drugs, keep hazardous or explosive materials (beyond normal household products), or damage the property. Tenant will follow Landlord's reasonable written rules, which are part of this Lease. No waterbeds or liquid furniture without written consent.

8. Pets and Smoking

  • No pets of any kind, even temporarily, unless a written pet addendum is signed by both parties. Service and assistance animals for a person with a disability are allowed as required by fair housing law, with no pet fee or pet rent.
  • No smoking or vaping of any product (including tobacco and marijuana) inside the unit or shed or within 20 feet of doors and windows. Tenant pays the cost of removing smoke odor or residue.

9. Maintenance and Repairs

Landlord is responsible for:

  • All exterior maintenance (roof, exterior walls, grounds, parking, common areas).
  • Interior repairs costing more than \$250.
  • Regardless of cost, every repair needed to keep the Premises fit and habitable (A.R.S. §§ 33-1324 and 33-1315): plumbing, water heater, heating and air conditioning, electrical, gas, the landlord-supplied refrigerator, microwave, and gas range, roof and structural items, pests or bed bugs not caused by Tenant, and anything required by building, health, or safety codes. Landlord also supplies running water and reasonable hot water and trash receptacles.

Tenant is responsible for:

  • Minor interior repairs and routine upkeep costing \$250 or less that are not habitability or code items, such as light bulbs, smoke-detector batteries, minor caulking, loose knobs or towel bars, screens, blind slats, and drain clogs caused by Tenant's use.
  • A/C filters: Tenant replaces the HVAC filter (size 12 x 24 x 1) monthly, at Tenant's expense. Damage caused by neglected filters is Tenant's responsibility. Otherwise, Landlord remains responsible for A/C repairs.
  • Any damage caused by Tenant or guests, at any cost, beyond normal wear and tear (A.R.S. § 33-1341).
  • Keeping the Premises clean, safe, and sanitary, and promptly reporting any defect or dangerous condition. Tenant may be charged for added damage caused by a delay in reporting.

Rules for work: No gas, electrical, or structural work by Tenant. No alterations, painting, lock changes, or alarm installs without Landlord's written consent; Tenant gives Landlord a key to any approved new lock. Tenant will test smoke detectors monthly and never disable them.

10. Vehicles and Parking (included in rent)

  • Parking. Tenant gets one (1) parking space in the front of the property, the space closest to Unit 1.
  • Work truck. Tenant may park his work truck (2007 Ford F250) at the very back of the property, provided it never blocks the ingress or egress of Landlord, Landlord's vehicles, or any other resident or vehicle, at any time. Repeated violations are a breach of this Lease.
  • Parking is for Tenant's use only and may not be rented or loaned.
  • Vehicles must be operable, registered, and insured. Inoperable or abandoned vehicles may be towed at the owner's expense after notice as allowed by law.
  • No commercial repairs or major mechanical work on site (minor upkeep like fluids or a tire change is fine). Tenant pays to clean oil or fluid stains.
  • No fuel cans, chemicals, or gas tanks stored in vehicles or parking areas beyond normal vehicle use. Tools stay secured in the truck or shed. No business operations from the truck at the property.
  • Tenant gives written notice before replacing either vehicle (year, make, and model).

11. Storage Shed (Tenant-built)

Tenant may build a storage shed at Tenant's own cost. The size, design, and location must be approved by Landlord in writing before building. Tenant keeps the shed safe, locked, and in good repair. Not allowed: hazardous or flammable materials, or living or sleeping in it. Landlord is not liable for the shed's contents. At move-out, Tenant removes the shed and restores the area, unless Landlord agrees in writing to keep it, in which case it becomes Landlord's property at no cost to Landlord.

12. Landlord Entry

Except in an emergency, Landlord will give at least two (2) days' notice and enter only at reasonable times to inspect, repair, or show the Premises (A.R.S. § 33-1343). Routine inspections are limited to once a month. Tenant will not unreasonably refuse entry, and Landlord will not use entry to harass Tenant. Tenant will tell Landlord in advance of any absence over 7 days (A.R.S. § 33-1373).

13. Insurance and Liability

Landlord's insurance does not cover Tenant's belongings, vehicles, or shed contents. Landlord is not responsible for loss of Tenant's property except loss caused by Landlord's negligence. Renter's insurance is strongly recommended.

14. Default, Abandonment, and Termination

  • Unpaid rent: 5-day written notice to pay or the Lease ends (A.R.S. § 33-1368(B)).
  • Breach affecting health and safety: 5-day written notice to fix.
  • Other material breach: 10-day written notice to fix.
  • Material and irreparable breach (such as violent or drug-related crime): immediate termination as allowed by A.R.S. § 33-1368(A).
  • A material misstatement on Tenant's rental application is grounds for termination.
  • Abandonment is determined under A.R.S. § 33-1370 (generally rent unpaid 7 days with no reasonable sign of occupancy, or 5 days with substantially all belongings removed), after the required posted and mailed notice. Left-behind property (including in the shed) is handled under § 33-1370, including the 14-day holding period.
  • Landlord will not lock Tenant out, shut off utilities, or remove belongings to force a move (A.R.S. § 33-1367). If Tenant moves out early, Tenant owes rent until the end of the term or until the unit is re-rented, whichever comes first; Landlord will make reasonable efforts to re-rent.
  • If Landlord cannot deliver possession for reasons beyond Landlord's control, Tenant may terminate and receive back all money paid.

Attachment B: Bed Bug Information (A.R.S. § 33-1319)

  • What they are: small, flat, reddish-brown insects about the size of an apple seed that hide in mattress seams, furniture, and cracks and bite at night.
  • Signs: itchy bites in lines or clusters, small dark spots on bedding, shed skins, or live bugs.
  • Prevention: inspect used furniture, mattresses, and clothing before bringing them in; check luggage after travel; reduce clutter; wash and dry bedding on high heat.
  • Report right away to Landlord in writing, text, or email. Do not use foggers or pesticides on a serious infestation. Tenant will allow access and follow treatment preparation instructions.
